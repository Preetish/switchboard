# Code of conduct

Switchboard follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas
rather than people, and help keep this a harassment-free space for everyone.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately
([@Preetish](https://github.com/Preetish)). Reports are handled confidentially,
and the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant apply.
